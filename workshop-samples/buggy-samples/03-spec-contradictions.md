## 1. Overview

The notification system shall deliver timely, relevant notifications to users across all platforms. Users should receive important updates immediately while respecting their preferences and avoiding notification fatigue.

---

## 2. Functional Requirements

### 2.1 Notification Delivery

**REQ-001:** The system SHALL deliver notifications within 30 seconds of the triggering event.

**REQ-002:** Notifications SHALL be batched and delivered every 5 minutes to reduce system load and prevent notification fatigue.

**REQ-003:** Critical notifications (security alerts, payment failures) SHALL be delivered immediately, bypassing any batching mechanisms.

**REQ-004:** The system SHALL support the following notification channels:
- Push notifications (mobile)
- Email
- In-app notifications
- SMS (premium users only)

### 2.2 User Preferences

**REQ-005:** Users SHALL be able to configure their notification preferences per channel.

**REQ-006:** Users SHALL be able to set "quiet hours" during which no notifications are delivered.

**REQ-007:** Critical notifications SHALL always be delivered, regardless of user preferences or quiet hours.

**REQ-008:** Users SHALL have full control over all notifications they receive.

### 2.3 Notification Types

**REQ-009:** The system SHALL support the following notification types:
- Informational (low priority)
- Action Required (medium priority)
- Critical (high priority)
- Marketing (lowest priority)

**REQ-010:** Marketing notifications SHALL only be sent to users who have opted in.

**REQ-011:** All users SHALL receive weekly product update notifications to stay informed about new features.

### 2.4 Rate Limiting

**REQ-012:** Users SHALL NOT receive more than 10 notifications per hour to prevent fatigue.

**REQ-013:** The system SHALL deliver all notifications generated by user actions without any rate limiting.

**REQ-014:** When rate limits are reached, notifications SHALL be queued and delivered in the next available window.

---

## 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Performance

**REQ-015:** The notification service SHALL handle 10,000 notifications per second at peak load.

**REQ-016:** The notification service SHALL scale horizontally to handle any load.

**REQ-017:** The system SHALL maintain 99.99% uptime (maximum 52 minutes downtime per year).

**REQ-018:** During maintenance windows, the system may be unavailable for up to 4 hours per month.

### 3.2 Data Retention

**REQ-019:** Notification history SHALL be retained for 90 days.

**REQ-020:** Users SHALL be able to access their complete notification history at any time.

**REQ-021:** Notification data older than 30 days SHALL be archived to cold storage and may take up to 24 hours to retrieve.

### 3.3 Security

**REQ-022:** All notifications SHALL be encrypted in transit and at rest.

**REQ-023:** Notification content SHALL be included in email subject lines for user convenience.

**REQ-024:** Sensitive notification data (account balances, transaction details) SHALL never be exposed in previews or subject lines.

---

## 4. Integration Requirements

### 4.1 Third-Party Services

**REQ-025:** The system SHALL integrate with SendGrid for email delivery.

**REQ-026:** The system SHALL NOT depend on any third-party services for core functionality.

**REQ-027:** Email delivery SHALL be handled entirely by the internal email service.

### 4.2 API Requirements

**REQ-028:** The notification API SHALL be RESTful and follow OpenAPI 3.0 specification.

**REQ-029:** All API responses SHALL be returned within 100ms.

**REQ-030:** The API SHALL support batch operations for sending up to 1000 notifications in a single request, with response time proportional to batch size.

---

## 5. Compliance Requirements

**REQ-031:** The system SHALL comply with GDPR requirements, including the right to be forgotten.

**REQ-032:** Complete audit logs of all notifications SHALL be retained for 7 years for compliance purposes.

**REQ-033:** When a user exercises their right to be forgotten, all their data including notification history SHALL be deleted within 30 days.

---

## 6. User Stories

### US-001: Immediate Critical Alerts
As a user, I want to receive critical security alerts immediately so that I can take action to protect my account.

### US-002: Peaceful Evenings
As a user, I want to set quiet hours so that I'm not disturbed by notifications during family time.

### US-003: Complete Control
As a user, I want full control over what notifications I receive so that I only see what's relevant to me.

### US-004: Never Miss Important Updates
As a user, I want to always receive important updates so that I never miss critical information about my account.

---

## 7. Acceptance Criteria

- All notifications delivered within SLA timeframes
- Zero critical notifications lost or delayed
- 100% of user preferences respected
- System handles peak load without degradation
